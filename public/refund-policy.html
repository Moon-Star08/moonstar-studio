Refund &
Cancellation

Last updated · 21 August 2026

This page explains how to cancel a care plan, what happens after you cancel, and when refunds do and don't apply — for both monthly care plans and one-time website builds. We aim to keep this simple and fair.

01 / Cancelling a care plan

How to cancel a care plan.

You can cancel a monthly care plan at any time by emailing moonstarstudio.co@gmail.com from the address on your account. (A self-serve cancellation option in your account portal is coming soon.)

To avoid being charged for the next month, please cancel at least 3 days before your renewal date. Cancellations made inside that window take effect from the following billing cycle.

02 / After you cancel

What happens after cancelling.

When you cancel, your care plan continues to the end of the month you've already paid for, and then stops. You keep all plan benefits — monitoring, backups, updates, and support — right up to the end of that paid period. Your website stays online; only the ongoing care service ends.

03 / Partial months

Partial-month refunds.

Care plans are billed monthly in advance and are non-refundable for the current month. We do not provide partial or pro-rated refunds for time remaining in a month — instead, you keep full access until the end of the period you paid for.
04 / One-time builds

Refunds on website builds.

Our one-time build packages (LaunchPad $299, GrowthSite $499, EmpireSite $949) are custom work, so refunds depend on the stage the project has reached:

  • Before design work starts: your deposit is refundable, minus any payment-processing fee already incurred.
  • Once design work has started: the deposit becomes non-refundable, as time and design work have been committed to your project.
  • After a stage is approved: amounts paid for completed and approved stages are non-refundable.
  • Final balance: due before the finished website is launched or handed over.

If you're unhappy at any stage, tell us — we would much rather work with you to put it right than have you feel stuck.

05 / Response time

How quickly we respond.

We answer all refund and cancellation requests within 5 business days. Approved refunds are issued through Paddle to your original payment method, and may take a few additional days to appear depending on your bank or card provider.
06 / Disputes

Questions or disputes.

If something doesn't look right on an invoice or charge, please contact us first at moonstarstudio.co@gmail.com before opening a dispute with your bank — we can almost always resolve it faster directly. This policy works alongside our Terms of Service.
